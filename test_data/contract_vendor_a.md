**3.1 Developer Rate.** Software Developer services are billed at no more than $85.00 per hour, capped at 160 billable hours per calendar month.
**3.2 Architect Rate.** Senior Solutions Architect services are billed at $120.00 per hour, capped at 80 billable hours per month.
**3.3 Project Manager Rate.** Project Manager services are billed at $95.00 per hour, capped at 80 billable hours per month.
**3.4 Consultant Rate.** Technical Consultant services are billed at $100.00 per hour, capped at 40 billable hours per month.
**3.5 Out-of-Schedule Services.** Any service not listed in the Rate Schedule requires prior written approval from Client before it may be invoiced.

## 4. Discounts

**4.1 Volume Discount.** When the monthly invoice subtotal exceeds $10,000.00, Vendor shall apply a 5% volume discount to the subtotal.

## 5. Taxes

**5.1 Sales Tax.** Sales tax of 8% applies strictly to the invoice amount AFTER deduction of the volume discount. Tax on the pre-discount subtotal is not permitted.

## 6. Payment

**6.1 Payment Terms.** Undisputed invoices are payable Net 30 from the invoice date.

## 8. Reconciliation

**8.1 Rounding Tolerance.** Variances of $1.00 or less per item are treated as rounding and are not a breach.
